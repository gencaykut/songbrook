Songbrook

A free, open-source YouTube Music player for Mac, iPhone and Android. Search, stream, download and keep your own library, without ads.

Latest release on GitHub

Download

macOS

macOS 12 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

  1. Open the disk image.
  2. Drag Songbrook to Applications.

Signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

Download for Mac

iPhone

iOS 15 or later · not on the App Store

  1. Download the IPA file.
  2. Install it with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore, signing in with your Apple ID.

With a free Apple ID the app must be refreshed every 7 days; AltStore and SideStore do this for you.

Download IPA

Android

Android 7 or later · not on Google Play

  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow your browser to install apps when asked.

To get updates automatically, add Songbrook to Obtainium.

What it does

  • The YouTube Music catalogSearch songs, albums, artists and playlists, with radio and suggestions on Home.
  • Downloads and a local librarySave songs for offline listening, with playlists, favorites and synced lyrics.
  • Songs, not music videosA music video plays its song version when there is one, and skips its non-music parts otherwise.
  • Songbrook ConnectControl your Mac from your iPhone, or move playback between them, on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Steady volumeLoud songs are lowered to an even level, as on YouTube Music.
  • Your history stays on your deviceMost played lists, skip-aware suggestions, and your top songs kept downloaded on Wi-Fi.
